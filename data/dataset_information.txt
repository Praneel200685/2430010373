Dataset  : ECG Heartbeat Categorization Dataset (MIT-BIH Arrhythmia part)
Source   : Kaggle - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shayanfazeli/heartbeat
Origin   : PhysioNet MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database (Moody & Mark, 2001), preprocessed by Fazeli
Files    : mitbih_train.csv (87,554 rows), mitbih_test.csv (21,892 rows)  -> 109,446 beats
Format   : 188 columns, no header. Columns 0-186 = ECG samples (187 points, scaled 0-1); column 187 = label
Classes  : 5 (AAMI EC57): 0=N, 1=S, 2=V, 3=F, 4=Q
HOW TO USE: download the two CSVs from Kaggle and place them in this folder (data/). They are not bundled
            in the ZIP to keep it small.
